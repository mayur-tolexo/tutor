An Armstrong number equals the sum of its digits each raised to the power of the number of digits. 153 = 1³ + 5³ + 3³ and 9474 = 9⁴ + 4⁴ + 7⁴ + 4⁴.

Read a positive integer and print `Armstrong` or `Not Armstrong`.

**Input:** one line.
