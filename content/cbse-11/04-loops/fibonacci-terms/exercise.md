Read `n` (1 or more) and print the first `n` terms of the Fibonacci series on one line, separated by single spaces. The series starts `0 1 1 2 3 5 8 …` — each term is the sum of the previous two.

**Input:** one line — `n`.

**Output:** the terms on one line.
