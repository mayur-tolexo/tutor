Read two positive integers and print their greatest common divisor (GCD) and least common multiple (LCM).

Tip: Euclid's method — repeatedly replace `(a, b)` with `(b, a % b)` until `b` is 0; then `a` is the GCD. LCM = (first × second) / GCD.

**Input:** two lines.

**Output:** two lines:
```
GCD: <gcd>
LCM: <lcm>
```
