Read the records of `n` students into a dictionary keyed by roll number, then print the names of students whose marks are above 75, in the order they were entered.

**Input:** first line `n`; then `n` lines, each `roll name marks` separated by spaces (names have no spaces).

**Output:** one name per line. If nobody scored above 75, print `None`.
