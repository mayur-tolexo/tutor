Read `n` numbers and print their sum and average. Compute the sum with a loop, not `sum()`.

**Input:** first line `n`; second line `n` integers.

**Output:** two lines:
```
Sum: <sum>
Average: <average>
```
The average is printed as Python prints a float (for example `5.0`).
