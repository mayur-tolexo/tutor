Read `n` numbers into a list and print the largest and smallest. Do it with a loop and comparisons, not `max()`/`min()`.

**Input:** first line `n`; second line `n` integers separated by spaces.

**Output:** two lines:
```
Largest: <x>
Smallest: <y>
```
