Read a year and print `Leap` if it is a leap year, else `Not leap`.

A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except years divisible by 100 — unless they are also divisible by 400. So 2000 and 2024 are leap years; 1900 and 2023 are not.

**Input:** one line.

**Output:** `Leap` or `Not leap`.
