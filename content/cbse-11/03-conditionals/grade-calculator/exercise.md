Read marks (0–100) and print the grade:

| Marks | Grade |
|---|---|
| 90 and above | A |
| 75 to 89 | B |
| 50 to 74 | C |
| below 50 | F |

**Input:** one line — the marks.

**Output:** the grade letter.
